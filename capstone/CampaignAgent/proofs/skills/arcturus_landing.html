Frequently Asked Questions

                    
Find answers to the most common questions about Arcturus and how it can transform your development process.

                

                

                    

                        

                            What kind of coding agents does Arcturus provide?
                            
                        

                        

                            
Arcturus provides a range of specialized agents, including code generation agents, testing and debugging agents, deployment agents, and refactoring agents. Each is designed to automate specific parts of the software development lifecycle.

                        

                    

                    

                        

                            How does Arcturus integrate with existing workflows?
                            
                        

                        

                            
Arcturus is built for seamless integration. It can connect with popular IDEs, version control systems (like Git), CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitHub Actions), and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) through APIs and webhooks.

                        

                    

                    

                        

                            Is my code secure with Arcturus?
                            
                        

                        

                            
Absolutely. Security is a top priority. Arcturus uses industry-standard encryption, strict access controls, and offers options for on-premise deployment to ensure your intellectual property remains private and protected.

                        

                    

                    

                        

                            Can I customize the agents for my specific needs?
                            
                        

                        

                            
Yes, Arcturus agents are highly customizable. You can train them on your specific codebase, define custom rules, and configure their behavior to align with your project's coding standards and requirements.

                        

                    

                    

                        

                            What kind of support is available?
                            
                        

                        

                            
We offer various support tiers, from community forums for our Starter plan to priority email/chat support for Pro, and a dedicated support manager with SLA guarantees for Enterprise clients.
